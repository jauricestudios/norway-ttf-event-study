# TTF daily-price provenance and event-clock audit (2026-11-05)

**Status: partially identified; NOT cleared for announcement-time inference.** This note audits what is verifiable from the published notebooks, the 2026-10-06 research report, and public vendor/exchange product pages. It is *not* a re-execution of the private CSV or a verification of the complete 2024–2026 contract history.

Date tests and OHLC/percent-change reconciliation do **not** identify roll jumps. | **Observational daily vendor-return differences only.** Roll discontinuities, changing contract maturity and session timing may contaminate event or benchmark returns. |
